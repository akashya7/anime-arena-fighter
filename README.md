# NEON DOJO — Grounded 2D Fighter

The game now uses a more grounded anime-fighter presentation rather than a cute cartoon style: taller human proportions, sharper silhouettes, restrained facial expressions, cloth folds, clothing details, combat stances, guard poses, hit reactions, stage lighting, and animated movement.

The three original fighters take broad visual cues from the supplied portraits—sari styling, white shirt and glasses, and blue shirt and glasses—but are fictional stylized adaptations, not exact likenesses. The art is original Canvas artwork and is not copied from Street Fighter or another commercial game.

## Controls

- **A / D** move
- **W** jump
- **J** punch
- **K** kick
- **Hold L** guard
- **U** special attack
- **Esc** pause

Choose a fighter, rival, difficulty, and arena before starting. Touch controls appear on smaller screens.
